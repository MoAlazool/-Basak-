Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen tap «نسيت كلمة المرور» and enter your number; the Basak team sends you a recovery code.

Receipt rejected

The reason is shown on the subscription page. Upload a new receipt from the same page.

No line for my university

Only lines that serve your university are shown. Contact a bus company in your area, or write to us.

Not receiving notifications

Make sure notifications for Basak are allowed in your phone settings, then open the app once while online.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account. Details on the delete account page.
