Rules that make it Flutter-safe

Direction
EdgeInsetsDirectional, AlignmentDirectional, TextAlign.start. No left or right anywhere.
Numbers
Western digits; times and phones wrapped in Directionality.ltr.
Blur
No BackdropFilter in lists or bars; it is the costliest effect on mid-range Android.
Shadows
Two only: card (0 1 2, 5%) and floating (0 12 32, 20–45%).
Icons
Lucide through app_icons.dart; new glyphs are added to the tree-shaken font.
Checks
Golden test per component at 360 and 390 wide, scale 1.0 and 1.3.

Layout

Screen padding
20 at the sides; 16 between sections
Card padding
18 to 20; 16 inside rows
Tap target
48 × 48 minimum, even when the glyph is 20
Text scale
Honoured from 1.0 to 1.3, then clamped
Smallest phone
360 × 640: nothing clips, grids keep 3 columns
Safe areas
Top 60 on the boards stands for the status bar
