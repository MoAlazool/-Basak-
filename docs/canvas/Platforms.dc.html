What the design does
Safe areas
Dynamic Island / notch on top, home indicator below
Status bar, then gesture line or three buttons
One SafeArea per screen. The 60 at the top of every board stands for it; the tab bar adds the bottom inset itself.
Going back
Swipe from the screen edge
System back gesture or button, with the predictive preview
Every pushed page keeps its round back button. Back closes a sheet first. A receipt being uploaded asks before leaving (PopScope).
Sheets
Swipe down to dismiss
Swipe down or system back
One BasakSheet for both: grabber, drag, scrim tap, back.
Keyboard
Rises over the screen
Resizes or pans the screen
The field in focus scrolls into view and the primary button rides on top of the keyboard. Phone fields open the number pad.
Notifications
One system question, once
A runtime permission since Android 13
The app explains first in its own card, then lets the system ask. If refused: the card in Alerts with "فتح الإعدادات".
Camera and photos
Usage text shown by the system
Runtime permission; the photo picker needs none
Asked only at the moment of use: the receipt, the profile photo, the supervisor's scanner.
Biometrics
Face ID or Touch ID
Fingerprint or face
Named after what the phone has. The password is always visible.
Wallet
Apple Wallet
Google Wallet
The official "Add to" button of each; the generic button on the boards is a placeholder.
Rating and update
App Store
Google Play
The same sheets; only the store's name changes.
Sharing and saving
Share sheet; saving to Photos asks once
Share sheet; saving needs no permission on current versions
Receipt PDF, receipt image and recap poster all leave through the system share sheet.
Haptics
Precise on every model
Vary by device, sometimes absent
Never the only signal: a scan result always has a colour, an icon and words.
Text size
Dynamic Type
Font size and display size
Honoured from 1.0 to 1.3, clamped above. Nothing depends on a fixed height of text.
Status bar icons
Dark or light per screen
The same, plus the navigation bar colour
Dark icons on the ground; light on the card, the scanner and the recap (SystemUiOverlayStyle).
Scrolling
Bounces
Stretches
Left to each platform. Pull to refresh on Home, Subscription, Card and Alerts.
Smallest screen
iPhone SE, 375 × 667
360 × 640
Checked at 360 wide: grids keep three columns, the card's QR shrinks last, long names end in an ellipsis.
Dark mode
System setting
System setting
Not offered yet. The app stays light in both; the tokens are named so a dark set can be added later.
Orientation and tablets

Rotation, tablets, foldables
Portrait only. On wide screens the content column stops at 480 and centres.
