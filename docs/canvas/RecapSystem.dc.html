Term recap · how one is built

A recap is assembled, not written: pages switch on and off with the data, every number has a singular, dual and plural, and every joke has a version for the student who rode twice and the one who never missed a day.

Their week, not the calendar's

Rule
Meaning
Which days are theirs
A weekday counts when they rode it in at least a third of the weeks since their first ride. Official holidays from the company's settings are left out.
Attendance
Rides on their days ÷ those days in the term. Three days a week and never absent is 100%, not 50%.
Tiers and titles
All measured on their days. «عمدة» needs a week of 4 days or more; 3 or fewer with 80% is «جدول على المقاس».
The weekday joke
Only about the weakest of their own days, and only when it is well below the best. Never about a day they do not come.
Runs and gaps
Counted in their days: Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday for three weeks is a run of 9.
Extra days
A ride outside their days is a bonus line («ويومين زيادة من عندك»), never a broken pattern.

When the data is thin or odd

Case
What the recap does
No college on the account
Greeting and college page fall back to the university. Nothing looks missing.
Never returned by bus
The return page becomes «تذكرة ذهاب بس»; no zero is shown as a failure.
One time all term
Favourite-time page becomes «معاد واحد طول الترم».
Subscribed mid-term
Percentages count from the first ride, not from the term's first day.
Changed stop or line
Station page uses the stop with the most rides and says «أكتر محطة».
Hours not available
Hours page and every hours line are left out; the recap still has nine pages.
A tie (two top days, two top times)
The earlier one wins; the copy never claims "the only".
Very long names
Stop, college and university names wrap to two lines; the title shrinks one step.

The voice

Rule
Meaning
Laughs with, never at
Jokes are about the road, sleep and the alarm clock.
Never about
Grades, money, a rejected receipt, the company, the driver or other students.
No gender
Every line reads the same to any student; no «إنتَ / إنتِ».
Only their own data
No ranking against others and no "top rider" claim.
Honest numbers
Hours are marked approximate. Counts are what was confirmed in the app.
Theirs to share
Nothing is posted by the app. The poster shows first name, line and Basak.app only.
